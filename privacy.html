DoctorCall Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

DoctorCall is a phone directory for South African public hospitals. It lists ward and department numbers, and which clinician currently holds each on-call role, so that hospital staff can reach the right person quickly.

DoctorCall holds no patient information of any kind. There is nowhere in the app to record a patient, a diagnosis, a case or a clinical note, and no such field exists in our database. The app is a contact directory for staff.

Who we are

DoctorCall is operated from South Africa and is subject to the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Questions, requests and complaints: bennokuhn1@icloud.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Your name — shown to colleagues at your hospital when you hold an on-call role, so they know who they are calling.
  • Your mobile number — confirmed by SMS, and shown to colleagues at your hospital only while you hold an on-call role. This is the number they dial. It is never shown to anyone at a different hospital.
  • Your professional registration number (HPCSA or SANC) — used once, to confirm you are registered. It is not shown to other users.
  • Your hospital — determines whose directory you see and who can see you.
  • Ward and department numbers you add — shared with everyone at that hospital. This is the directory itself.

Verifying that you are a clinician

When you register with a professional registration number, we ask you to photograph an identity document. This is handled by Didit, an identity verification provider, who read the name from the document and confirm it is genuine.

We compare that name against the public register published by the Health Professions Council of South Africa, and keep only the outcome: whether the registration was found, whether it is active, and the name on it. We do not keep a copy of your identity document, and it is never shown to other users.

We do this because the app publishes your phone number to your colleagues, and they need to be able to trust that the person listed as the on-call registrar really is a registered clinician.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not record your location. The app requests no location permission.
  • We do not record who you call. Calls are placed by your own phone, and we never see them.
  • We do not hold patient information.

Who can see what

Your name and number are visible only to verified staff at your own hospital, and only while you hold an on-call role. Ward and department numbers are visible to anyone signed in, because a ward's landline is not private information and nobody should be blocked from reaching a hospital.

These limits are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.

Where your information is stored

On servers operated by Supabase, and with our identity verification provider for as long as a verification takes to complete. Where that involves a transfer outside South Africa, it is made under section 72 of POPIA on the basis that the receiving country provides comparable protection.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, for as long as you keep it.
  • Records of on-call shifts, briefly, so that a disputed handover can be resolved. We deliberately do not keep a long-term record of where you have been or when.
  • The outcome of your registration check, so we do not have to repeat it.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it.

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — open Account at the top of the main screen. This removes your name, your number and any on-call role you hold, immediately and without asking us.

Ward and department numbers you contributed remain in the directory. They describe the hospital rather than you, other staff rely on them, and removing them would take working numbers away from a hospital because one person left.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

Children

DoctorCall is for healthcare staff and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than only changing this page.
