DoctorCall Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

DoctorCall is a phone directory for South African public hospitals. It lists ward and department numbers, and which clinician currently holds each on-call role, so that hospital staff can reach the right person quickly.

Do not enter patient information. DoctorCall is a contact directory for staff, not a patient-record system. We do not request patient records, diagnoses or clinical notes. Listing text and reports must not contain that information; report any inappropriate entry.

Who we are

DoctorCall is operated from South Africa and is subject to the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Questions, requests and complaints: bennokuhn1@icloud.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Your name — shown to colleagues at your hospital when you hold an on-call role, so they know who they are calling.
  • Your mobile number — confirmed by SMS, and shown to colleagues at your hospital only while you hold an on-call role. This is the number they dial. It is never shown to anyone at a different hospital.
  • Your professional registration number (HPCSA or SANC) — used once, to confirm you are registered. It is not shown to other users.
  • Your hospital — determines whose directory you see and who can see you.
  • Ward and department numbers you add — shared with everyone at that hospital. This is the directory itself.
  • Push notification token — stored only if you allow notifications, so we can alert you when an on-call role changes hands.
  • Crash diagnostics — technical error details are sent to Sentry when the app fails, including when browsing without an account. We disable session replay and breadcrumbs. JavaScript reports exclude raw error messages, request contents, account details and search text; they retain limited technical details used to diagnose failures.

Directory reports

Account blocks store the two account identifiers and the time the block was created. Only the person setting a block can read their block list in the app. Blocks prevent direct handovers between those accounts and hide directory descriptions on listings attributed to a blocked original contributor or a server-recorded editor. The app keeps a private account-scoped copy of affected listing identifiers for offline use. Clinical contact numbers and essential shift alerts remain available. They do not block telephone calls. Blocks remain until removed in the app or either account is deleted. A local copy is cleared when you sign out.

For new directory-description edits, we record the editing account, affected listing and field, and edit time to support blocking and moderation. These attribution records do not duplicate the description text or telephone number. They are available to authorized moderators, not other staff. A field’s record is replaced when that field changes and is removed when its listing or editor account is deleted. Older edits may not have reliable attribution and require manual review.

If you report a directory listing in the app, we store your account identifier, the listing identifier, the selected reason, the time and the review status. Reports are available to you and authorized reviewers, not other users. These account-linked reports are removed when your profile is deleted. Email reports are handled through our support mailbox. Do not include patient information, identity documents or private phone numbers in reports.

Identity verification

When you register with a professional registration number, we ask you to photograph an identity document. This is handled by Didit, an identity verification provider, which processes the document and returns a verification result.

The document must be scanned live with your phone's camera; uploaded photos are not accepted. While you scan it, Didit also uses your front camera to capture your face and compares it with the photo on the document, to check that the person holding the document is its owner. This comparison involves biometric information, which POPIA treats as special personal information, and it is done only with the consent you give in Didit's verification screen. If the comparison is inconclusive, your verification is held for manual review instead of being approved automatically. DoctorCall receives only your first and last name and the verification result; we never receive your document images or your face image.

We compare the result with your professional registration. Our account records include verification status, relevant name and registration results, provider session references and operation records needed to complete verification or deletion. We do not copy identity-document images into our directory database, and your document is not shown to directory users. Provider processing and storage are separate from what the directory database stores.

We do this because the app publishes your phone number to your colleagues, and they need to be able to trust that the person listed as the on-call registrar really is a registered clinician.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app does not request GPS location permission. Identity-verification providers may process IP addresses and associated network or approximate-location information for verification and fraud checks.
  • We do not record who you call. Calls are placed by your own phone, and we never see them.
  • We do not provide a patient-record system. Do not enter patient information into directory contributions or reports.

Who can see what

Verified staff can find colleagues' names when arranging a handover. A live role publishes its listed contact number to verified staff with access to that hospital. Other users cannot browse your private profile or professional registration details. Ending a role removes its number from subsequent live directory updates; a device that is offline may retain its last saved copy until it reconnects. Ward and department numbers are visible without signing in, because a ward's published institutional number is not personal information and nobody should be blocked from reaching a hospital. Guests cannot see profiles, personal mobile numbers, on-call roles, audit identities or unpublished directory entries.

These limits are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.

Where your information is stored

Account and directory information is stored on servers operated by Supabase. Identity verification is processed by Didit. Push delivery is processed by Expo and Apple, and crash diagnostics are processed by Sentry. These providers receive only the information needed to provide their service and are required to protect it. Where processing involves a transfer outside South Africa, it is made under section 72 of POPIA on the basis that the recipient provides comparable protection.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, for as long as you keep it.
  • Records of on-call shifts are retained with your account for resolving disputed handovers. Ended shifts are not available in other staff's live directory. These records are removed when your profile is deleted.
  • The outcome of your registration check, so we do not have to repeat it.
  • Identity verification data held by Didit, including document and face images, for up to one month, after which Didit deletes it. Deleting your account also asks Didit to delete your verification session.
  • Push tokens until you delete your account or the token is replaced.
  • Crash diagnostics according to Sentry's configured retention period.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it.

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — open Account at the top of the main screen. This removes your name, your number and any on-call role you hold from our active account database after deletion succeeds. Deletion first requests removal of verification data and can remain pending if the provider cannot confirm it or an identity check is still finishing. Contact our support address if retrying does not resolve the problem. Other devices update their saved copies when they next synchronize. If deletion fails, the app tells you rather than claiming that your account was removed.

Ward and department numbers you contributed remain in the directory. They describe the hospital rather than you, other staff rely on them, and removing them would take working numbers away from a hospital because one person left.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

Children

DoctorCall is for healthcare staff and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than only changing this page.
