DoctorCall Terms of Use

Last updated 7 September 2026

What DoctorCall is

A phone directory for South African public hospitals. It lists ward and department numbers, and which clinician currently holds each on-call role.

DoctorCall is not a medical device and gives no clinical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor or measure anything. It holds no patient information. It is a directory of telephone numbers, and nothing in it should be relied on as clinical guidance.

It is not an emergency service

DoctorCall does not place calls, does not route emergencies, and cannot guarantee that anyone listed will answer. In an emergency use your hospital's own emergency procedures and switchboard.

Numbers may be wrong

Ward and department numbers are contributed by the staff who use the app. We do not independently verify them, and hospitals change extensions without telling anybody. Every listing shows when it was last confirmed, and a listing that has not been confirmed recently is marked as such — please read that before relying on it, and correct anything you find to be wrong.

Whether a clinician is genuinely on duty is likewise what they told the app. We show how recently it was confirmed; we cannot know where anyone actually is.

Who may use it

Healthcare staff at South African hospitals. Registering requires a valid HPCSA or SANC registration number, or being vouched for by two verified colleagues at your hospital.

What you agree to

  • That the registration number you enter is your own. Using someone else's is a serious matter and we will remove the account.
  • Not to add anyone else's personal number to the directory. Your own number is published only by you, only while you hold an on-call role, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Not to enter patient information anywhere in the app. There is no field for it and no legitimate reason to try.
  • To use the directory for reaching colleagues at work — not to collect numbers, to market anything, or for any purpose unrelated to clinical work.

Ward numbers you contribute

Numbers you add to the directory stay there if you leave. They describe the hospital rather than you, and other staff come to rely on them. Your personal details and any on-call role are removed with your account.

Availability

DoctorCall is provided as it is. We do not promise it will always be available or always correct. The app keeps a copy of the directory on your phone so that it still works without signal, but that copy can be out of date and says so.

Ending your use

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — open Account on the main screen. We may remove an account that uses someone else's registration, publishes other people's numbers, or enters patient information.
